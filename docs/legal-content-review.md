## Privacy

Prepare a collection notice identifying the responsible legal entity, contact channel, purposes, required/optional fields and intended recipients. Inventory the data actually processed: Auth email/session/MFA records; profile/contact/address data; cart/wishlist/order snapshots; payment references and refund evidence; internal notes/audit entries; email delivery metadata; manual communication records; exported reports; and operational logs. Do not claim card data is stored when payments are hosted.

Document processors and cross-border arrangements, retention/deletion rules, access/correction/objection requests, identity verification, breach handling and separation of marketing consent from transactional messages. Explain essential cookies and local preferences accurately; do not add a decorative consent banner for nonexistent analytics. These decisions require owner and appropriate legal review under [POPIA](https://www.justice.gov.za/legislation/acts/2013-004.pdf), including collection notification and security safeguards.

## Trading terms and disclosures

Verify the legal business identity, service address, business email and applicable registration disclosures before publishing final terms. Explain products/pack units, authoritative prices, non-VAT status as supplied, payment methods, order acceptance, delivery eligibility, collection arrangements, cancellation and complaints. Do not invent bank details, map coordinates, delivery boundaries or guarantees.

Review online transaction disclosures and customer review/correction opportunities against [ECTA](https://www.gov.za/documents/electronic-communications-and-transactions-act). Review quality, defective goods, complaints and refund terms under the [Consumer Protection Act](https://www.gov.za/documents/consumer-protection-act). Do not use blanket “no refunds” wording for perishable products or assume a single cooling-off rule applies to every transaction. Legal review must determine the applicable rules and exceptions for this store.
