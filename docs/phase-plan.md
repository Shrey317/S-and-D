# Approved phase plan

The owner approved Phases 1 and 2 on 14 October 2026, Phase 3 on 16 October 2026, and Phase 4 by replying “continue” to its explicit approval request. Hosted services remain deferred; Phase 4 uses real local Supabase Auth with captured email alongside the isolated PostgreSQL catalog. Phase 5 was approved on 17 October 2026. Phase 6 was authorized by the subsequent “continue” and resumed on 19 October 2026. Phase 7 was explicitly approved with “continue to phase 7”. Phase 8 was approved by “continue” in response to the explicit Phase 8 request, and resumed on 20 October 2026. Phase 9 was explicitly approved with “continue phase 9” on 20 October 2026. No public deployment or genuine payment activation without explicit owner approval.

## Final route map (planned, not implemented)

Phases 1–8 are complete locally; the [Phase 8 verification record](phase-8-verification.md) captures the current build and service limitations. The route map below remains the full planned scope, including routes not yet implemented.

Public: `/`, `/shop`, `/shop/[slug]`, `/cart`, `/about`, `/contact`, `/privacy-policy`, `/terms-and-conditions`, `/delivery-and-collection`, `/returns-and-refunds`, `/cancellation-policy`.

Authentication: `/auth/sign-in`, `/auth/register`, `/auth/verify`, `/auth/forgot-password`, `/auth/reset-password`, `/auth/error`, callback handler `/auth/callback`.

Customer: `/checkout`, `/checkout/confirmation/[orderId]`, `/payments/[paymentId]/status`, `/account`, `/account/profile`, `/account/security`, `/account/orders`, `/account/orders/[orderId]`, `/account/addresses`, `/account/wishlist`, `/account/privacy`.

Administrator: `/admin`, `/admin/products`, `/admin/products/new`, `/admin/products/[productId]`, `/admin/categories`, `/admin/inventory`, `/admin/inventory/history`, `/admin/orders`, `/admin/orders/[orderId]`, `/admin/refunds`, `/admin/customers`, `/admin/customers/[customerId]`, `/admin/promotions`, `/admin/promotions/new`, `/admin/promotions/[promotionId]`, `/admin/reports`, `/admin/notifications`, `/admin/communications`, `/admin/contact-submissions`, `/admin/audit-logs`, `/admin/privacy-requests`, `/admin/content`, `/admin/settings`.

Framework/support: robots, sitemap, not-found and error pages; Payfast ITN/browser return endpoints, Resend events, authorized upload signatures, exports and non-sensitive health checks. Customer details require authenticated ownership, even when IDs are unguessable. No unauthenticated order-confirmation link exposes personal data.
